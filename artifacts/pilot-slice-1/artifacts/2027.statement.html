Objective

Two players alternately remove letters from a row of coloured pieces under a strict neighbour condition; decide whether the first player (Alice) wins when both play optimally.

Operation

Alice moves first and may remove a piece coloured A only when both of the pieces immediately beside it are also coloured A. Bob may remove a piece coloured B only when both pieces immediately beside it are also coloured B. Players alternate; a player unable to make a legal removal loses immediately.

Input

The method receives one parameter colors, a string consisting solely of the letters A and B. The first and last pieces can never be removed because they do not have a piece on both sides.

Output

The method returns a boolean: true when Alice wins, false when Bob wins.

Constraints

  • The length of colors is between 1 and 100 000 characters inclusive.
  • Each character is either A or B.

Edge Cases

  • The first and last pieces can never be removed because they lack two neighbours.
  • A player may only remove a piece of their own colour, never the other player's.

Load‑bearing rule

Both neighbours must match the piece’s colour; a single matching neighbour is insufficient.

Method behaviour

The method accepts the string colors and returns a boolean. It does not print and does not return a count of moves.
