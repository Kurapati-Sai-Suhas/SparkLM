Two players, Alice and Bob, play on a row of coloured pieces represented by a string of the letters A and B. Alice moves first and may remove a piece coloured A only when both of its immediate neighbours are also A. Bob may remove a piece coloured B only when both neighbours are B. The first and last pieces can never be removed because they do not have a piece on both sides. Players alternate turns; if a player has no legal move they lose immediately. The game always starts with the given row and the goal is to decide whether Alice can force a win when both play optimally.

Input: a single parameter colors – a string of length between one and 100000 containing only the characters A or B.

Output: a boolean value – true if Alice wins, otherwise false (Bob wins).

Constraints: the string length is at most 100000, and it contains only A and B. No other characters are allowed.

Edge cases: the first and last pieces are never removable; a player may only remove a piece of their own colour; both neighbours must match the piece’s colour, not just one.

Each player may remove at most one piece on their turn.

def removeColoredPiecesIfBothNeighborsAreTheSameColor(self, colors: str) -> bool

Example

colors = "AABAA"
# Alice can remove the middle A (its neighbours are both A), leaving "ABAA".
# Bob has no B with two B neighbours, so he cannot move and loses.
# The method returns true
