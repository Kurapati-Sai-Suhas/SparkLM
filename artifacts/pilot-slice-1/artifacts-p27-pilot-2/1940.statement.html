You are given a string s consisting of lowercase English letters and an integer k. First, replace each letter by its position in the alphabet (a = 1, b = 2, …, z = 26) and concatenate the positions to form a decimal number. Then, perform exactly k rounds; in each round replace the current number by the sum of its decimal digits. After the final round output the resulting integer.

Constraints:

  • The length of s is between 1 and 100 inclusive.
  • k is between 1 and 10 inclusive.
  • The conversion concatenates positions, so a letter whose position has two digits contributes two digits.
  • Rounds continue even when the number becomes a single digit; further rounds leave it unchanged.

def sumOfDigitsOfStringAfterConvert(self, s: str, k: int) -> int:

Example

For s = "az" and k = 1:

  • Convert: a → 1, z → 26, concatenate → "126".
  • Round 1: sum of digits 1 + 2 + 6 = 9.

The method returns 9.
