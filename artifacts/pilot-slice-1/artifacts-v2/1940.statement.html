Given a string s consisting of lowercase English letters and an integer k, first replace each letter by its position in the alphabet (a=1, b=2, …, z=26) and concatenate those positions to form a decimal number. Then perform exactly k rounds; in each round replace the current number by the sum of its decimal digits. After the final round output the resulting integer.

Constraints: 1≤|s|≤100, 1≤k≤10.

Even if the number becomes a single digit before all rounds are finished, continue the rounds; summing the digits of a single‑digit number leaves it unchanged.

Note that a letter whose position has two digits (i.e., ten through twenty‑six) contributes two digits to the concatenated number.

def sumOfDigitsOfStringAfterConvert(self, s: str, k: int) -> int

Example

s = "abc"
k = 2
# conversion: a→1, b→2, c→3 → "123" → number 123
# first round sum of digits: 1+2+3 = 6
# second round sum of digits: 6
output = 6
