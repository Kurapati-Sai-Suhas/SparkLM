Two players, Alice and Bob, take turns removing pieces from a row of coloured pieces represented by a string of the letters A and B. There are two players, and Alice moves first. Alice may remove a piece coloured A only when both of the pieces immediately beside it are also A. Bob may remove a piece coloured B only when both neighbours are B. The first and last pieces can never be removed because they do not have a piece on both sides. Each player may only remove pieces of their own colour. Players alternate; a player who cannot make a legal remove loses immediately. The task is to decide whether Alice wins when both play optimally.

Input: a single parameter colors, a string of length between one and 100000, each character being either A or B.

Output: a boolean value – true if Alice wins, otherwise false.

Constraints: the string contains only the letters A and B; length ≤ 100000.

Edge cases: the first and last pieces are never removable; a player may only remove a piece of their own colour.

Example

colors = "AABAAAB"

Alice can remove the middle A (its neighbours are both A), then Bob has no legal move, so the result is true.

def removeColoredPiecesIfBothNeighborsAreTheSameColor(self, colors: str) -> bool
